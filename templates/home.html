The Recipe column decides what each row shows, and each account is remembered separately; Recipes for all accounts above sets every one of them at once and reads Mixed while they differ. Tick none and the row shows every recipe with the closest match marked; tick one and the row shows that recipe alone; tick several and each keeps its own line, its own percentage and its own counts, which is how you read an account against this month's baseline and the one it is moving away from at the same time. Nothing is re-compared whichever you choose — every account is measured against every recipe in the library, so the figures are already worked out. Choosing does check that account against OpsRamp straight away, ahead of the routine refresh, and rewrites the figures only if something has actually moved.

no assets means the account has nothing in that category. no target in place of a percentage means the account has equipment there that no recipe names — a gap in the recipe, not in the tool; open the cell to see which models. no version means OpsRamp reported no firmware version for them — a gap in the inventory. Either way a percentage would be misleading, so none is shown, and the cell still links to the rows behind it. no target on one line means that particular recipe has nothing to say about this equipment. The grid is cached, so it is here the moment you sign in and survives a restart; the figures are from the time shown above, not from this instant. What was read from OpsRamp is kept separately from what it was measured against, so adding or removing a recipe re-measures every account from what has already been read — in seconds, and without touching OpsRamp. Only the background scan reads the estate again, and it leaves accounts whose firmware has not moved exactly as they were.
